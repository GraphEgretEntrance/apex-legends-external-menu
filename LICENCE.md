## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Menu
- ESP — outlines and boxes for enemy legends
- Aim Assist — adjustable aim smoothness and FOV
- Recoil Control — reduce weapon kick on all guns
- Radar — 2D minimap with enemy positions
- Item ESP — highlight loot, ammo, and hop-ups

### 🎨 Visuals & Overlay
- Skeleton ESP — draw bones for visible targets
- Health Bars — show HP and shields above enemies
- Distance Indicators — display range to each target
- Stream-Proof Overlay — hide the menu on capture

### 🏋️ Utility & Extras
- Spectator Warning — notify when being spectated
- Instant Reload — skip reload animations
- No Spread — tighten bullet spread
- Bunny Hop Helper — maintain speed during jumps
